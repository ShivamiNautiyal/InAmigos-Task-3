Who We Are

A campus built for curiosity, character & confidence

Since 2001, Springfield Public School has grown from a single block of classrooms into a full-fledged CBSE campus that welcomes over 5,000 students every year. We believe learning is most powerful when it is joyful — so our classrooms are designed for discussion, discovery and hands-on inquiry, not just note-taking.

Our Mission — to nurture independent thinkers who are academically strong, emotionally grounded, and prepared to contribute meaningfully to the world beyond the classroom.

Our Vision — to be recognised as a school where every child is known by name, challenged by design, and supported without exception — building a community that values curiosity as much as achievement.

From the Principal's desk — "Education here is a partnership between teachers, parents and students. We don't just prepare children for exams — we prepare them for the questions life hasn't asked yet." — Dr. Anjali Mehra, Principal

Why Springfield

Six reasons parents trust us with their children

Every decision on campus — from classroom design to bus routes — is made with one question in mind: is this best for the child?

Experienced Faculty

250+ trained educators, many with 10+ years of classroom experience and ongoing pedagogy training.

Smart Classrooms

Interactive boards and digital content bring every subject to life, from geometry to geography.

Sports Excellence

State-level champions in athletics, football and swimming, coached on our own dedicated complex.

Digital Learning

A blended-learning platform lets students revisit lessons, submit work and track progress online.

Safe Campus

CCTV-monitored premises, trained security staff and a dedicated medical room, always on standby.
